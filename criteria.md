# Acceptance criteria — The Unofficial Guide

Five criteria that say what "working" means for this system, written in unit 1
**before** any results existed.

An acceptance criterion names a target: a number, a count, a rate, or something
a person could plainly observe. *"Retrieval works"* is an opinion. *"For at
least 4 of my 5 test questions, the top results include a chunk containing the
answer"* is a criterion.

Under each one, write a sentence or two on **why that target** and not a
stricter or looser one. A reason that says something about your corpus or your
pipeline earns credit; *"80% seemed reasonable"* does not.

> Missing your own targets next unit costs you nothing. Setting a target so
> easy you can't miss it does.

---

## 1. Retrieved chunks contain the answer

For at least 5 of my 5 test questions, the retrieved chunks include one that
contains the answer.

**Why this target:**
Each individual question references only one text document. Due to the nature of this structure, this metric will prove that the system is correctly identifying the text document needed to answer the question. If it does not, this proves the chunking strategy needs to be fine-tuned to create a stronger semantic signal.

## 2. Every answer names a source

Every answer the system produces names at least one source document.

**Why this target:**
<!-- Why all five and not four? What about your setup makes that achievable —
     or what would have to go wrong for it not to be? -->
Every answer should be derived from the documents within the Corpus. To visibly validate that the answer is derived in the manner explained, the answer should include the source document. If it does not, it is a strong indicator that the answer might be a hallucination.

## 3. The relevance gate stops out-of-corpus questions

When I ask a question my documents clearly don't cover, the relevance gate
stops it and the system returns "I don't have enough information about that" —
in at least 5 of 5 tries.

**Why this target:**

This target focuses on the accuracy of the responses. By choosing a 5 out of 5 metric, we are validating that the responses are accurate, are not hallucinations and are grounded on real data.

## 4. Something about your chunks

For all responses, the chunk size is between 50 to 200 characters. 


**Why this target:**

The corpus contains severtal text documents containing a question and 2-7 responses. Each question and response is 1-2 sentences in length. Due to the short nature of this corpus, the response should mirror that short length as well. This ensures that the response is not to verbose and utilizes the data from the text documents.

When given a question with subquestions, the response contains an answer for all the subquestions asked. 


**Why this target:**

The metric validates the relevance of the response. It is a strict all or nothing metric that ensures the answer to any given question is complete and answers everything the user is inquirying about.
